The telescope

The object stays fixed. Value collapses face cards to 10. Rank separates them. Color introduces the signed −1/+1 coordinate. Suit finishes the identity.

For candidate counts m, the increments Δ=(1/m_after)−(1/m_before) always sum from 1/3 to 1. That is the telescoping ledger; it is not automatically a probability model.

The invariant marker

The 51 active cards contain 25 black and 26 red identities. With black −1 and red +1, their signed total is +1. Outside marker Ⅰ=2♣ contributes −1 and restores zero.

The marker balances the physical pack but never enters a round.

Archive-addressing corollary

One equal-weight fiber can address the verified archive.

The v7.2 contribution profile contains a class of 18 distinct cards with exactly equal component weight. Hold its 18 occupied positions—and every card outside that class—fixed. Permuting only those 18 identities gives 18! = 6,402,373,705,728,000 arrangements with the same complete position-by-position weight profile.

14! = 87,178,291,200 < 103,800,788,359 < 15! = 1,307,674,368,000 ≤ 18!

Therefore every one-based index in the verified LMFDB archive can be injected into this single observational fiber. The cards carry an address; recovering an ordinate still requires the declared factorial ordering, the index convention, and the external archive. This does not compute a zero, extend the verified height, or imply RH.
