How jbits work

One bank. Clear prices.

jbits are the lab’s play currency. Start with 8. The reserve recovers 1 every 5:10 only while your available bank plus active finance holdings are below 8; winnings can carry total value higher. Reloading and switching games preserve the bank.

ActionPrice or award
Blackjack stake1, 2, 4 or 8 jbits
One full hidden-card reveal6 jbits
Blackjack odds view / forecast entry1 jbit each
Correct four-choice Memory recall without a reveal+2 jbits; up to 8 per UTC day

These are declared prices. The reveal price of 6 follows the six-bit fixed-width budget for 51 identities; the recall award of 2 follows the two-bit budget for four choices. Neither converts play into a measurement of personal memory, surprise or learning. jbits have no monetary value.
