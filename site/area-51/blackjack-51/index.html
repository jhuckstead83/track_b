Table rules & a different experiment

This beta uses the existing Line Game cards. Change a rule between rounds to reshuffle. Your bank and history stay with you. The starting table uses shuffled order, flexible aces, and a dealer who stands on soft 17.

The same seed, rules and decisions reproduce the same session. Published order is a known sequence, not a random deal.

Applying rules keeps your jbits. Reloading resumes your hand and bank; it does not grant another starting balance.

  1. Get closer to 21 than the dealer. Busts lose immediately. Equal non-bust totals push. Suits do not break ties.
  2. Aces adapt to the whole hand. In the flexible rule, count an ace as 11 when the total stays at or below 21. Face cards count as 10.
  3. Natural blackjack pays 3:2. An unsplit opening ace and ten pays a 1½-stake profit. Ordinary wins pay a one-stake profit. Pushes return the stake.
  4. Hit, stand, or double. Double on the first two cards of a hand: add the same stake, draw exactly one card, and stand. jbits must cover the added stake.
  5. Split once into two hands. Matching blackjack values may split, including different ten-valued faces. Split aces get one more card each. A split 21 is an ordinary 21; no resplit.
  6. The dealer has a fixed rule. Draw below 17. The default stands on soft 17. Dealer blackjack is checked before player decisions. No insurance or surrender.
  7. The shoe is finite. Cards stay out until the next shuffle. Fewer than 24 cards remaining triggers a fresh shoe before the next round. No mid-hand shuffle.
  8. Ⅰ changes what you know. One paid next-card peek per round costs 6 Area 51 jbits, with no change to order or blackjack payout. If you learned a future card earlier, that observation follows it into later rounds.
  9. Your bank belongs to Area 51. The shared reserve opens at 8 jbits and passively recovers one jbit every 5:10 while below 8. Blackjack winnings can take the wallet above the reserve cap.
  10. A reload keeps the same bank. Stakes, returns and unfinished hands are saved. Time away counts toward the refill cap. Loading a replay is read-only and cannot fund your bank.

All jbits are local play points. The 51-card pack and information peek are this game's additions; no casino strategy or optimal-payoff claim is made. Conventional blackjack reference.
