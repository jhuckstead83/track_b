Rule: three queues; the highest front card wins; the winner appends the played

- **Turn-85 present.** Four legal pasts. 01 and 11 are excluded at every turn: **A**. Past 10 is reached from 17–17–17 deals exactly at turns 24, 27, 30 and 33: **A-CAT**. So at turn 85 exactly one past comes from a fair deal.

  - they exist **iff 3 | n and n ≥ 12**: **A**, by construction plus the rigidity theorem;
  - their period is 3·lcm(k_A, k_B, k_C, k_A+k_B+1, k_B+k_C+1, k_C+k_A+1) or 3·lcm(k_A, k_B, k_C, K+1, K+2), depending on orientation: **A**;

  - No rigid loop is reachable from an equal deal, at any n: **A**.

# 4. STATUS LANGUAGE — permanent

EXACT            algebraic identity proved symbolically.
A                unconditional analytic theorem.
A-CAT            rigorous computer-assisted theorem, replayable certificate.
CANDIDATE A      apparently rigorous analytic result, one independent return,
                 awaiting another replay.
CANDIDATE A-CAT  rigorous-looking finite certificate, one return, awaiting
                 independent reproduction.
RH-EQUIVALENT    logically equivalent to RH. Not a proof in either direction,
                 and never progress.
B                useful partial theorem or lemma not closing its target.
C                numerical or suggestive evidence. Never upgrades a status.
O                open.
NO-GO            a proposed universal implication has been disproved. A closed
                 route is not a step toward a proof.

Concordance with the volume's words, since the two tiers differ:

  PROVED closed form -> EXACT        RH-EQUIVALENT -> RH-EQUIVALENT
  PROVED analytic    -> A            EVIDENCE      -> C
  CERTIFIED A-CAT    -> A-CAT        OPEN          -> O

Concordance with the Area 51 notes (51 SEDAPS, 51 Twins), which use their own
five words:

  PROVED   -> A (or EXACT)           EVID     -> C
  CERT     -> A-CAT                  OPEN     -> O
  COMPUTED -> A-CAT (exhaustive search resting on a proved lemma)

Do not invent status strings outside this list. If a claim does not fit, say
so rather than coining a word.

merely observed (PP284 §0, PP287 §5).
